# Border Studio

Border Studio is a Windows app for streamers who want their webcam to sit in a shape they chose, with a border that matches the rest of the stream. You draw the hole, pick how the edge looks, and OBS shows your camera through that hole with the border on top.

Open the Designer, shape the hole on the Mask tab, paint the edge on Border, and stash one-shot bursts on Effects. When you go live, OBS uses that look: your face in the cutout, the color and glow around it, effects when you fire them.

That is the whole job. Fancy frames without fighting scene collections by hand. Start with the [Quick Start](quick-start.md); the other pages go deeper when you want them.
